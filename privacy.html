Atlas Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 August 2026

Atlas is local-first. Recovery notes and other private records stay on this device unless you deliberately choose an optional cloud backup.

1. Local storage and optional cloud backup

Information you enter is saved in your device's browser storage and remains available without a cloud account. Cloud backup is optional and manual. If you create an Atlas Cloud account and choose Upload this device, supported recovery contacts, journal entries, mood history, milestones, safety-plan content, reflections and profile data are sent to a Supabase-hosted database. Atlas does not upload this information automatically. Choosing Restore from cloud replaces the corresponding local records with your cloud copy.

Supabase Auth processes the email address and password used for the cloud account. Atlas does not receive or store your plaintext password. Cloud records are protected by account authentication and database row-level security, but they are not end-to-end encrypted.

2. Analytics and advertising

Atlas does not use third-party analytics, behavioural tracking or advertising SDKs.

3. Location services

If you use the nearby coffee-shop feature, your browser may request location. The coordinate is used momentarily to query OpenStreetMap through the Overpass API. Atlas does not save, record or transmit your location history.

4. External services

Atlas links to official fellowship resources and embeds YouTube videos. Those services have their own privacy policies. Atlas does not pass your private Atlas records to them.

5. Deleting data

Use Atlas's clear-data control or clear browser site data to remove local records. This does not remove a cloud backup. Cloud account and backup deletion must be handled through the Atlas support contact or the Supabase project administrator.

6. Safety boundary

Atlas is a recovery-planning and continuity tool, not treatment, diagnosis, a clinical decision system, a professional record or emergency care. If there is immediate danger in the UK, call 999. Samaritans can be reached on 116 123; urgent medical help is available through NHS 111.

Contact

For general questions, use the official Atlas repository. Do not post private health or recovery information in a public issue. A dedicated privacy contact address and legal controller details must be added before a public store submission.
