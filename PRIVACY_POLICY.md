# Atlas Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 28 August 2026

Atlas ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use the Atlas application.

## 1. Local-First Data Storage
Atlas is a fully local-first application. **We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data, health information, journals, or recovery plans to any external servers.** 

All information you enter into Atlas is saved exclusively within your device’s local browser storage. We do not have a backend database, and we have no ability to see, access, share, or sell your data.

## 2. No Analytics or Tracking
We do not use any third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising SDKs (such as Google Analytics or Facebook Pixel) that monitor your behaviour within the app.

## 3. Location Services
If you use the "Find a nearby coffee shop" feature, your browser may ask for your location. This coordinate data is used momentarily by your device to query OpenStreetMap via the Overpass API. Atlas does not save, record, or transmit your location history.

## 4. External Links
Atlas includes links to official fellowship resources and embedded YouTube videos (such as Lavendaire guided meditations). When you click these links or play these videos, you are interacting with third-party services (e.g., Google/YouTube) which have their own privacy policies. We do not pass your Atlas data to these services.

## 5. Deleting Your Data
Because your data is stored locally, you have total control over it. You can permanently delete all Atlas data from your device at any time by using the "Clear this device" button within the app's Settings/Privacy view, or by clearing your browser's site data.

## 6. Peer Support Disclaimer
Atlas is built on lived experience and offers personal suggestions for recovery. It is not a clinical tool, medical device, or replacement for professional healthcare. No clinical records are generated or processed by this application.

## Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the official repository or support channels.
