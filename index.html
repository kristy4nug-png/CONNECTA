Get me through 20 minutes
20:00

1. Make the next few minutes safer

Move away from substances, weapons, traffic, unsafe people or anything you might use to harm yourself. Go somewhere more public or closer to a safe person when possible.

2. Slow the body down

Place both feet on the floor. Breathe in gently, then make the out-breath a little longer. Repeat without forcing it.

3. Contact one human being

Send a plain message: “I am struggling and need someone to stay in contact with me for twenty minutes.”

4. Delay the decision

You do not need to solve your life tonight. Agree not to use, leave, confront anyone or make a permanent decision until the timer ends.

5. Choose what happens next

Go to a meeting, call support, eat something, take prescribed medication, rest somewhere safe, or seek urgent professional help.

About

CONNECTA is a free, independent, private-first recovery planning and continuity tool for Windows. It is not treatment, diagnosis, a clinical decision system, a professional record or emergency care.

Privacy

Your entries are kept in this browser’s local storage on this device. CONNECTA has no cloud account and does not automatically send recovery information. Clearing browser site data may permanently remove it. A downloaded export may contain sensitive information; protect it carefully.

Using CONNECTA

CONNECTA is provided for voluntary personal planning. Check appointments, meeting details, travel, services and medical information with the responsible official source. Do not rely on CONNECTA where delay or error could cause harm. You remain in control of what you enter and share.

Support

If there is immediate danger in the UK, call 999. Samaritans can be reached free on 116 123. For urgent medical help that is not an emergency, use NHS 111. CONNECTA cannot monitor messages or call for help.
