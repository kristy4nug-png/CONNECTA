BUILD CONNECTA AS A RESPONSIVE FLUTTERFLOW WEB AND MOBILE APP

PROJECT
Name: CONNECTA
Tagline: Together as one
Purpose: A recovery safety and continuity app for people in recovery, rehabilitation,
healthcare, prison release and community resettlement in the UK.

DESIGN
Use the supplied green CONNECTA icon and lock-up.
Primary dark green: #355846
Secondary green: #5F8167
Warm amber: #E3A53C
Soft sage: #E1EBDD
Warm white: #F8F8F3
Main text: #1F2933
Use large accessible text, generous spacing, rounded cards and calm non-institutional screens.
No neon colours, shame-based streaks, surveillance imagery or punitive language.

BOTTOM NAVIGATION
Today | Meetings | Reflect | Tools | My Plan

CORE SCREENS
1. Welcome and safety check:
   I'm steady / I'm struggling / I don't feel safe.
2. Get me through 20 minutes:
   five-step coping flow, timer and emergency contact actions.
3. One-task mode:
   show only the next safe action.
4. Meetings:
   AA, NA and ACA official finder links plus a private personal meeting calendar.
5. Daily reflection:
   original recovery reading, private note, and link to official fellowship literature.
6. Quiet Room:
   YouTube-embedded Lavendaire meditation playlist with clear attribution.
7. Coffee shop finder:
   location permission, nearby café results, walking directions, no location storage.
8. Coping toolbox:
   HALT, grounding, urge surfing, pause, meeting preparation and boundaries.
9. Support ladder:
   ordered list of trusted contacts.
10. Service promises:
   what was promised, responsible service, due date and status.
11. Transition checklist:
   identification, phone, medication, accommodation, GP, benefits, bank, transport,
   food, probation/key worker, trusted contact and first meeting.
12. Privacy:
   clear consent, export and delete controls.

FREE PROTOTYPE RULES
Use local state and test data only.
Do not store real health, prison, safeguarding or identifying information.
Do not add paid APIs.
Play videos through YouTube. Do not copy or rehost audio.
Open official fellowship sources rather than reproducing protected literature.
Make all screens responsive for mobile and desktop.
