# Atlas

Atlas is a local-first recovery-support application that helps people access practical support, continuity and resettlement resources without shame-based messaging.

## Language

**Recovery Fellowship Finder**:
The single Atlas area for finding and organising AA, NA and ACA meetings. New meeting capabilities extend this area rather than creating a second meeting section.
_Avoid_: Meeting Hub, separate meeting section

**Published Meeting**:
An AA, NA or ACA meeting whose shared listing comes from an authorised fellowship data source and may change when meeting information is refreshed. Published Meetings are a future capability until such a source is available.
_Avoid_: Official Meeting, Atlas Meeting

**Official Fellowship Link**:
A clearly labelled external link to a meeting finder or contact service maintained by AA, NA or ACA. Atlas does not copy or claim ownership of the information shown there.
_Avoid_: Atlas meeting search, imported meeting

**Personal Meeting**:
A user-controlled meeting record containing their own saved details, plans or reminders. It is never overwritten by a refresh of published meeting information.
_Avoid_: Published Meeting, synced meeting record

**Favourite Meeting**:
A user-saved reference to a Published Meeting. It remains visible with a warning if its published listing later becomes unavailable.
_Avoid_: Personal Meeting, guaranteed meeting
