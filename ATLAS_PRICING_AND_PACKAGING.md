# Atlas pricing and packaging

Status: working proposal only, 4 August 2026. These figures have not been approved or made live.

## Free (£0 forever)

All safety, urgent help, core plans, check-ins, journal, contacts, appointments, meeting/support finder, Recovery Capital, Worker Handover, basic export/backup/restore/delete, offline use and accessibility remain free. No adverts, data sale, worker/employer sharing or payment requirement.

## Proposed launch pricing (central configuration required)

| Offer | Proposed price | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Atlas Plus | £4.99/month VAT included; £39.99/year VAT included | convenience/enhancement only: sync, encrypted backup, advanced insights, owned/licensed content, reports and bounded supporter sharing |
| Pilot | £1,500 + VAT / 12 weeks | one site, 100 active users, 15 staff; possible annual credit |
| Team | £299 + VAT/month | 100 active users, 15 staff, one site |
| Organisation | £999 + VAT/month | 500 active users, 75 staff, five sites |
| Enterprise | from £24,000 + VAT/year | custom use, SSO, multi-site administration, onboarding, SLA, security documentation and priority support |

No number is to be scattered through client code. Pricing, VAT display, trial, cancellation, refund and entitlement configuration belong in a server-controlled configuration after legal/commercial approval.

## Evidence and recommendation

No authoritative, like-for-like public UK pricing for recovery-support software was identified in this initial audit; enterprise healthcare pricing is commonly quote-led and is not sufficient evidence to revise the supplied prices. Keep the requested figures as proposed launch prices. Before a live recommendation, obtain at least five dated competitor quotes or public price pages, interview target buyers, model support/onboarding/hosting/insurance costs, and test willingness to pay with a non-binding pilot proposal. Do not silently change the supplied figures.

## Cost forecast — unavoidable categories

- Public release: domain, HTTPS hosting/CDN, transactional support email, backups and monitoring.
- Paid service: payment processor fees, VAT/accounting, secure hosting/database/object storage, identity/SSO, support and incident response.
- Assurance: solicitor/data-protection advice, DPIA support, clinical-safety professional, accessibility audit, independent penetration testing, Cyber Essentials assessment, insurance.
- Stores: Apple Developer Program and Google Play Console accounts; confirm current fees before purchase.

Exact amounts require vendor, region, usage, tax and account decisions; no spend is authorised by this document.
